Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Weather Summary handles information when you use this website.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described on this page.

Information You Provide

Weather Summary does not require you to create an account to search for weather information.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information contained in your message, including your email address and any information you choose to provide.

Location Information

The website can use your browser's location feature when you select "My Location".

Your browser will ask for permission before providing location information to the website.

If you do not want to provide your location, you can use the city search instead.

Weather Summary does not require your precise location in order for you to use the city-search feature.

Weather and Location Services

Weather Summary uses Open-Meteo services to retrieve weather and geocoding information.

When the website requests weather information, relevant location coordinates are sent to the weather service so that forecast data can be returned.

More information can be found on our Weather Sources page.

Cookies

The core Weather Summary website does not require cookies to provide basic weather search and forecast functionality.

Third-party services that may be added to the website in the future, such as advertising or analytics services, may use their own technologies subject to their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Weather Summary may display advertising in the future to help support the website.

If advertising services are used, those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not sell personal information directly to advertisers.

External Links

The website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

Please review the privacy policies of external websites before providing them with personal information.

Children's Privacy

Weather Summary is a general information website. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's services, features or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
