Privacy Policy

Welcome to Weather Summary. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website.

Weather Summary is designed to provide weather information, forecasts and location-based weather searches. We aim to collect and use only information that is necessary for the website's functionality and services.

Last updated: August 30, 2026

🔎 City Searches

Weather Summary allows users to search for weather information by entering a city or location.

When you search for a location, the search information is used to request the relevant geographic coordinates and weather data from the third-party services used by the website.

Weather Summary does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to perform a normal city search.

📍 Browser Location Permission

Weather Summary may provide a My Location feature that allows you to request weather information for your current location.

If you use this feature, your web browser may ask for permission to access your device's location.

Location access is controlled by your browser and device. You can choose whether to allow or deny this permission.

You can continue using the website's city search feature without granting browser location permission.

🌦️ Weather API Requests

Weather Summary uses third-party weather and geocoding services to obtain weather forecasts and location information.

When you request weather information, your browser may make requests to these external services in order to retrieve the required data.

Our primary weather data provider is Open-Meteo.

For more information about the weather data used by this website, please visit our Weather Sources page.

🍪 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Weather Summary does not intentionally use cookies to identify individual users for its basic weather-search functionality.

However, third-party services used by the website may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies according to their own policies.

If Google advertising or other third-party services are enabled on this website, those services may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

📢 Google Advertising

Weather Summary may display advertisements through Google AdSense after advertising is enabled.

When Google advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable settings, policies and user choices.

The use of advertising technologies may allow information about a user's interaction with websites or advertisements to be processed by Google or its partners.

Users can learn more about Google's advertising practices and available privacy controls through Google's own privacy and advertising settings.

Important: Advertising and consent requirements can vary depending on the user's location and applicable laws. Where required, Weather Summary will use appropriate consent and privacy controls for applicable users.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Weather Summary may interact with third-party services to provide website functionality.

These may include:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecast data and related weather services.
  • Geocoding services — used to convert searched place names into geographic coordinates.
  • Google services — may be used for advertising and related services after advertising is enabled.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. Weather Summary does not control how independent third-party services process information.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers.

📊 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Weather Summary does not require users to create an account, provide their name, or submit personal information in order to use the basic weather-search features.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the normal weather-search functionality of the website.

Information processed by third-party services, such as information associated with API requests, advertising, security, or browser functionality, may be subject to the policies of those services.

🛡️ Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and its services functioning securely. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should understand that information transmitted over the internet may be subject to risks outside our control.

👨‍👩‍👧 Children's Privacy

Weather Summary is a general informational website and does not knowingly request personal information from children through its basic weather-search functionality.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted through the website inappropriately, please contact us.

📧 Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the operation of Weather Summary, you can contact us through our Contact page.

When contacting us, please provide enough information for us to understand and respond to your request.

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's features, services, advertising technologies, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
