💬 What You Can Contact Us About

You can contact us regarding topics such as:

  • Problems using the weather search
  • Issues with the My Location feature
  • Website errors or broken pages
  • Suggestions for new features
  • Feedback about the website
  • Questions about our weather data sources
  • Questions about our Privacy Policy
  • Questions about our Terms of Use

🐛 Reporting a Website Problem

If you notice a technical problem, please include a short description of the issue in your email.

If possible, also tell us which page or feature caused the problem. This can help us understand and investigate the issue.

Tip: Please do not include passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal information in your email.
