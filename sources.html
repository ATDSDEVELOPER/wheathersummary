🌤️ Weather Data

Weather Summary uses Open-Meteo to retrieve weather forecast information.

Depending on the requested location and available data, the service provides information such as temperature, humidity, wind, precipitation, pressure, weather conditions, sunrise and sunset.

Open-Meteo documentation:

📍 Location Search

City and location searches are handled using the Open-Meteo Geocoding API.

The geocoding service converts a city or location name into geographical coordinates that can be used to request weather information.

📊 Weather Variables

Weather Summary may display the following information obtained from the weather service:

  • Air temperature
  • Apparent or feels-like temperature
  • Relative humidity
  • Wind speed
  • Precipitation probability
  • Precipitation amount
  • Atmospheric pressure
  • Weather condition codes
  • Sunrise and sunset

⚠️ Forecast Limitations

Weather forecasts are produced using weather models and available observations. Forecast information can change as new data becomes available.

Weather Summary does not guarantee that forecast information is accurate, complete or suitable for a particular purpose.

🚨 Official Weather Warnings

Weather Summary does not provide an official emergency warning service.

For severe weather, tropical cyclones, flooding, lightning, dangerous winds or other emergencies, please consult the relevant official authorities in your country or region.
