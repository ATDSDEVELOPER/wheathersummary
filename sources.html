🌤️ About Our Weather Data

Weather Summary uses external weather data services to provide current weather information and weather forecasts for locations searched by users.

Primary data provider: Weather Summary uses Open-Meteo weather and geocoding services to provide weather forecasts and location-based weather information.

🌦️ Open-Meteo

Open-Meteo provides weather forecast data that can be used by applications and websites to display weather information.

Weather Summary retrieves weather-related information through the Open-Meteo services and presents selected information in a simple and user-friendly format.

Open-Meteo

Official website: https://open-meteo.com/

Open-Meteo API documentation: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs

📍 Location and Geocoding Data

When a user searches for a city or location, Weather Summary uses geocoding services to identify the corresponding geographic location and coordinates needed to retrieve weather information.

The location information is then used to request weather data for the selected location.

Search results may vary depending on the information available from the geocoding service.

📊 Weather Information We Display

Depending on the selected location and available data, Weather Summary may display information such as:

  • Current temperature
  • Feels-like temperature
  • Humidity
  • Wind speed
  • Wind direction
  • Atmospheric pressure
  • Precipitation probability
  • Rain and precipitation information
  • Hourly weather forecasts
  • Daily weather forecasts
  • Sunrise and sunset times
  • General weather conditions

⚠️ Weather Data Accuracy

Weather forecasts are generated using weather models and available meteorological data. Weather conditions can change quickly, and forecast information may differ from actual conditions.

Weather Summary does not guarantee that all weather information displayed on the website will always be completely accurate, current, or available.

We aim to present available weather information clearly, but users should consider forecasts as informational rather than guaranteed predictions.

🚨 Emergency and Safety Information

Weather Summary is not an emergency warning service. Information displayed on this website should not replace official weather warnings, government alerts, or instructions from local authorities.

For serious emergency situations, severe weather events, or safety-critical decisions, users should rely on their local official authorities and emergency services.

🔄 Updates and Improvements

We may update the website's features, weather data presentation, and data sources from time to time in order to improve the reliability and usability of Weather Summary.

Changes to this page will be made when the services or data sources used by the website change.
