About Weather Summary

Weather Summary is a weather information website designed to provide current weather conditions, hourly forecasts and 7-day forecasts for locations around the world.

Weather information includes temperature, humidity, wind speed, precipitation probability, atmospheric pressure, sunrise and sunset times.

Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo. Forecast information should not be considered an official emergency warning.
