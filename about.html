About Weather Summary

Welcome to Weather Summary, a simple web-based weather information service designed to help users quickly check current weather conditions and forecasts for locations around the world.

Our goal is to make weather information easy to understand and accessible on both desktop and mobile devices.

Our goal: To provide a simple and convenient way for users to check current weather conditions, hourly forecasts and upcoming weather information.

🌦️ What We Provide

Weather Summary provides useful weather information including:

  • Current weather conditions
  • Temperature and feels-like temperature
  • Humidity information
  • Wind speed information
  • Chance of rain
  • Atmospheric pressure
  • 24-hour weather forecasts
  • 7-day weather forecasts
  • Sunrise and sunset information
  • Weather summaries for searched locations

📍 Location Search

Weather Summary allows users to search for weather information by entering a city or location.

The website may also use the browser's location feature when a user chooses the My Location option. Location access requires permission from the user's browser and is not required to use the normal city search feature.

🌐 Weather Data

Weather information displayed on Weather Summary is obtained from weather data services. The current website uses Open-Meteo as a weather data provider.

Weather conditions can change rapidly, and forecasts are based on available weather models and data. Therefore, weather information shown on this website should be considered informational and may not always perfectly represent actual conditions.

For severe weather, emergencies, or safety-critical decisions, users should also check official weather authorities and emergency services in their local area.

🎯 Our Approach

Weather Summary is designed with simplicity in mind. We aim to present useful weather information without making the experience unnecessarily complicated.

We continuously work to improve the website's functionality, performance, usability and accuracy of the information displayed.

⚠️ Important Information

Weather Summary is an informational website. Weather forecasts and other weather information may contain inaccuracies or may change as new weather data becomes available.

We do not guarantee that all weather information will be available, complete, accurate or continuously updated.

Users should not rely solely on this website for emergency, aviation, maritime, medical, or other safety-critical decisions.

📧 Contact Us

If you have questions, suggestions, or notice an issue with Weather Summary, please visit our Contact page.

About Weather Summary

Weather Summary is a web-based weather information service designed to provide easy access to current weather conditions and forecasts.


The website provides current weather information, hourly forecasts and seven-day forecasts for locations around the world.


Users can search for a city or use their browser's location feature to request weather information for their current location.


Weather information may include temperature, humidity, wind speed, precipitation probability, atmospheric pressure, sunrise and sunset times, and weather conditions.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make weather information simple, accessible and easy to understand.
