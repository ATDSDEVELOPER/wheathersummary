About Weather Summary

Weather Summary is a simple web application designed to make weather information easy to view and understand.

The website provides current weather conditions, hourly forecasts and a seven-day forecast for locations around the world.

What We Provide

  • Current temperature
  • Feels-like temperature
  • Weather conditions
  • Relative humidity
  • Wind speed
  • Precipitation probability
  • Atmospheric pressure
  • 24-hour weather forecast
  • Seven-day weather forecast
  • Sunrise and sunset times

How It Works

When you search for a location, Weather Summary first uses a geocoding service to identify the location and its geographical coordinates.

The website then requests weather forecast data for those coordinates and displays the results in an easy-to-read format.

Weather Data

Weather data is provided through Open-Meteo. For more information about the services and data used by this website, please visit our Weather Sources page.

Important Information

Weather forecasts can change as new observations and forecast model calculations become available. Weather Summary should therefore not be considered a replacement for official weather warnings, emergency instructions or professional advice.

For severe or dangerous weather, always check the official meteorological and emergency authorities in your area.
